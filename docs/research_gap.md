# Research Gap

This project investigates adaptive wireless-network optimization under dynamic simulation conditions.

Potential gaps to review with real literature in a later documentation phase:

- Generalization of ML resource-allocation decisions across traffic and interference conditions.
- Trade-offs between throughput, latency, energy, and spectrum efficiency.
- ML decision latency and computational overhead.
- Simulation-to-real-world transfer limitations.
- Robustness of classical ML methods compared with rule-based wireless baselines.

No specific paper claims are made in Phase 1. The literature review must be built from genuine papers and should record title, authors, year, method, simulation or dataset, findings, limitations, and relevance.
